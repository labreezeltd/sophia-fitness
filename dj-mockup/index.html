The artist

Ten years reading the floor

Kairo has spent a decade turning warehouses, terraces and cathedrals of sound into single, breathing organisms. What started as bedroom edits in East London is now a passport full of stamps and a signature blend of melodic techno and afterhours house.

Every set is built live, in the moment — no two nights the same. The through-line is feeling: hypnotic low-end, arms-up melodies, and that one drop the whole room remembers on the walk home.
