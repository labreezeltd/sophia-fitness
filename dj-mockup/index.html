The selector

From bedroom edits to the main stage

Josh "Moo" Marshall is a Manchester-based open-format DJ and UKG producer — a North West selector with a flavour for UK garage, disco, house and all things bass. What began as edits at home is now a full-time craft, built on reading a room and running it.

Co-founder and resident of Looney Grooves, the UKG & bass-heavy night out of Manchester, and a resident selector for Funk My Life bringing nu-disco to Leeds. Festival stages include Parklife 2022 & 2023 — proof the sound travels from basement to big top.
