# 🌺 Aloha Luau Co. — Promo Kit

Promotional package for the Hawaiian luau accessories (coconut bras, seashell
bras, rainbow leis, coconut cups). Three deliverables, ready to launch.

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| [`index.html`](./index.html) | A complete, conversion-focused **landing page** — single self-contained file, no build step. |
| [`ad-copy.md`](./ad-copy.md) | **Advert copy** for Meta, TikTok, Google, Pinterest, email & SMS. |
| [`ugc-content.md`](./ugc-content.md) | **UGC content** — short-form scripts, hooks, captions and a creator brief. |

## View the landing page
Open `marketing/index.html` directly in any browser — that's it. No dependencies.

## Make it yours (3 quick edits)
1. **Add your photos.** Each product card and the hero have a labelled image
   placeholder (a coloured block). Replace them with the 4 supplied product
   photos using `<img src="...">`.
2. **Wire up checkout.** The "Add to Cart" buttons carry `data-product` IDs and
   a placeholder click handler at the bottom of `index.html`. Point each one at
   its Shopify product/variant or checkout URL.
3. **Set your real offer.** Prices, the discount %, the announcement bar and the
   countdown timer are all placeholders — update them to match your actual deal.

## Notes
- The landing page is brand-agnostic; "Aloha Luau Co." is a placeholder name —
  swap it for your store's name in the nav, footer and `<title>`.
- Prices shown ($15.99–$39.99) are examples. Confirm against your store.
- Keep all claims (sizing, materials, shipping times, returns) truthful and in
  line with your actual policies before going live.
