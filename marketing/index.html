Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes do the bras come in?

Our coconut and seashell bras are one-size-fits-most with adjustable tie-back straps, comfortably fitting most cup sizes from A to DD. The straps tie at the neck and back so you can dial in your perfect fit.

What's included in each set?

It varies by product — check each listing. Sets typically include the bra plus a combination of a rainbow flower lei necklace, matching flower bracelets and a flower-topped coconut sipping cup. The full bundle includes both bras, two lei sets and two cups.

How fast is shipping?

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day. Standard delivery typically arrives in 3–5 business days, with express options at checkout. Every order is tracked, and shipping is free on orders over $35.

Are the coconut cups safe to drink from?

Yes! The coconut cups are food-safe and reusable. Hand-wash with warm soapy water between uses and they're ready for your next island night.

What's your return policy?

We offer 30-day easy returns on unused items in original packaging. If something isn't right, reach out and our team will sort it out, hassle-free.
