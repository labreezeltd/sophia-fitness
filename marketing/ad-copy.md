## Creative direction notes
- **Imagery:** use the 4 supplied product photos. Lead with the lifestyle/model
  shots for cold traffic; use flat-lay "what's in the box" shots for retargeting.
- **Format priority:** 4:5 (feed), 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Stories), 1:1 (carousel).
- **Carousel idea:** card 1 = coconut set, card 2 = seashell set, card 3 = the
  cups, card 4 = bundle "style your crew", card 5 = reviews screenshot.
- **Compliance:** keep claims truthful (sizing, materials, shipping times). Avoid
  before/after or unrealistic guarantees.
