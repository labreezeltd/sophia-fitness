# Aloha Luau Co. — UGC Content Pack

Ready-to-shoot scripts, hooks, captions and a creator brief for user-generated
content (TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts). UGC for these products wins
when it feels like a real friend showing off a party haul — not a polished ad.

Products featured: coconut bra set, seashell bra set, rainbow lei + bracelets,
coconut sipping cups, and the Ultimate Luau Bundle.

## B. Short-form scripts (15–30 sec)

### Script 1 — The Unboxing (TikTok / Reels)
**[0:00] Hook (talking to camera, holding mailer):**
"Okay, my luau kit just arrived and I'm opening it on camera so you see exactly
what you get."
**[0:03] B-roll — open box, pull items out one by one:**
VO: "Coconut bra… rainbow lei… matching bracelets… and an actual coconut cup."
**[0:10] Try-on / hold up to camera:**
"The straps tie at the back so it actually fits — I was worried about that."
**[0:16] Detail shots — shells, flowers, cup:**
"Colours are way brighter in person, and the cup is reusable which I love."
**[0:22] Payoff (to camera):**
"Whole look for under $20, here in 3 days. Luau = sorted. 🌴"
**[0:26] CTA:** "It's on sale right now — linked it for you."

### Script 2 — Get Ready With Me (GRWM)
**Hook:** "Get ready with me for a beach luau 🌺 using the set everyone's been asking about."
- Time-lapse: putting on the coconut/seashell bra, layering the lei + bracelets.
- Mirror selfie spin. "One-size, tie-back, didn't budge all night."
- Sip from the coconut cup to camera. "And yes, the cup works."
- End card: "Set's linked — currently 40% off."

### Script 3 — The "Me vs My Bestie" Bundle (duo content)
**Hook:** "We split ONE bundle for our girls' trip — coconut vs seashell, who wore it better?"
- Split screen / two friends: one in coconut set, one in seashell set.
- Quick cuts of both posing, laughing, holding coconut cups.
- "$39.99 for both of us. Cheaper than one costume-shop bra."
- CTA: "Bundle's in the link 👯‍♀️"

### Script 4 — Honest Review (builds trust)
**Hook:** "Honest review of the coconut bra set — no I'm not sponsored, I bought it."
- Pros to camera: "Comfy, adjustable, arrived fast, cup is a fun touch."
- One honest 'con' (authenticity!): "Wish it came in more shell colours."
- Verdict: "For a themed night out? 10/10, would buy again."
- CTA: "Linking it — grab it before the sale ends."

## E. Creator brief (send this to UGC creators / micro-influencers)

**Brand:** Aloha Luau Co. — tropical party essentials.
**Product:** [Coconut set / Seashell set / Bundle — specify per creator]
**Deliverables:** 1× 15–30s vertical video (9:16) + 3× photos, raw + edited.
**Vibe:** Fun, authentic, "showing a friend my party haul." Natural lighting,
real reactions. Not overly polished or salesy.

**Must include:**
- A strong hook in the first 3 seconds (see section A).
- Clear unboxing OR try-on showing the bra, lei and coconut cup.
- Show the tie-back straps / mention one-size fit (top question from buyers).
- Verbal + on-screen mention of: ships fast, reusable, on sale.
- End with a CTA ("linked in my bio" / "use my code").

**Please avoid:**
- Unrealistic claims (fit guarantees, "best ever").
- Covering the product with filters/effects so it can't be seen clearly.
- Trademarked music if the video may be used in paid ads (use commercial-safe audio).

**Usage rights:** Requesting whitelisting / paid usage rights for 90 days across
Meta + TikTok ads. (Confirm rate and rights in your agreement.)

## F. Turning UGC into ads (the flywheel)
1. Seed product to 5–10 micro-creators + encourage buyer tags.
2. Collect best-performing organic clips.
3. Re-cut the top hooks into 9:16 paid ads (see `ad-copy.md`).
4. Layer captions/subtitles + a clear end card with the offer.
5. Scale the winners; refresh creative every ~2 weeks to fight fatigue.
