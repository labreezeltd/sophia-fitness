About Lexi Law

A modern firm, built on old-fashioned trust

Lexi Law Ltd is an independent solicitors' practice based in Stockport, serving clients across Greater Manchester and beyond. Founded in 2023 and authorised by the SRA, we set out to make quality legal advice approachable, transparent and genuinely human.

We believe good representation isn't about intimidating offices or baffling paperwork — it's about listening carefully, acting decisively, and treating every client the way we'd want to be treated ourselves.
